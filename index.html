    Your day

      Practical notes

      Getting around

      Most places are within a twenty-minute walk of the harbour. The Coast Loop bus leaves the ferry terminal every forty minutes.

      When to visit

      Spring and autumn are mild and quiet. Summer weekends fill up by eleven in the morning, so start early or go on a weekday.

      Good to know

      Many shops close on Sunday afternoons. Keep some cash for the market stalls and the harbour parking meter.
